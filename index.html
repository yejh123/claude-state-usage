Claude.ai use by state · May 2026

D.C. uses Claude.ai at 13 times West Virginia's rate

In May 2026, Washington, D.C., used Claude.ai at 3.3 times the rate its population would predict. West Virginia used it at a quarter of the expected rate.

Fifteen states and D.C. use Claude.ai more than expected. Thirty-four states use it less.

An index of 1.0 means a state's share of US Claude.ai use matches its share of US working-age people (ages 15 to 64). Illinois is at exactly 1.0.

      In high-use and low-use states alike, about 4 in 10 conversations are for work

      Anthropic labels a sample of Claude.ai conversations as work, personal use or coursework. In the 15 states and D.C. above 1.0, an average of 41% of conversations were for work. In the 34 states below 1.0, the average was also 41%. The two ends of the ranking are close too: 44% in D.C. and 41% in West Virginia.

      Across all 50 states and D.C., the work share stays between 36% (Delaware) and 48% (Florida). States differ far more in how much they use Claude.ai than in what they use it for.

      What this data can't tell us

      • It covers Claude.ai only, on the Free, Pro and Max plans. Use through the API or Claude Code is not included.
      • It covers one month, May 2026, so it can't show whether the gap is growing or shrinking.
      • It shows where use is high or low, but it doesn't explain why states differ.
